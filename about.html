About Me

Hi! I invite you to take a deep breath… like actually. I know it may seem silly, but the absence of a weightless breath is heavy. As an artist I strive to make the people around me consider the significance in what we deem trivial. Similar to how someone can be grateful for taking a breath when they are no longer on oxygen.

I want to be able to shine a light on the stories that never made it outside the dinner table, not because the stories weren’t worth sharing, but because someone before me deemed the person sharing the story not worthy. And in every performance I do I seek to bring value. Because there is something beautiful about being seen, heard, and understood.

And I want to be the vessel that can hold onto the complexity of being a human being and share that with people. So that even a silent whisper can ring alarms. And most importantly so that someone can see a vessel they look like, feel like, talk like, live like, and tell themselves “I am worthy”.

I aspire to bring love into everything I touch; a script, a guitar, a mic, a brush, an audience. A love that’s willing to be naked in the eyes of what they fear most and a love that feels like “Bachata Rosa” on a Sunday morning. I want the love I carry to overflow into my community and fill the space abundantly. Even if it manifests itself with a simple “good morning”.

A single blade of grass may seem invaluable by itself, but when placed in a grass field it becomes a shelter. And I wish to portray the importance of what it feels like to be a grass field to our communities, how together we have value.
